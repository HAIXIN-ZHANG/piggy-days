# Piggy Farm Game Design

## Goal

Make Piggy Farm feel like a small cozy game that grows from our real life.

The farm should make my wife want to open the app because it feels cute, personal, and alive. It should not become a stressful daily chore or a complicated farming simulator.

## Design Inspirations

### Ant Forest

Useful pattern:

- Real-life behavior creates virtual energy.
- Virtual progress becomes visible long-term impact.
- The app gives the user a clear sense that small actions matter.

Piggy Days adaptation:

- Real-life tasks, check-ins, outings, and grocery savings create farm resources.
- Farm growth visualizes our shared life, not carbon impact.
- A completed week should leave something visible behind, such as a flower bed, photo card, or tiny decoration.

### Ant Manor

Useful pattern:

- Simple care loop.
- Feeding creates a warm emotional reward.
- Public-good donation turns a small action into meaning.

Piggy Days adaptation:

- Feeding piggies should be a small happy tap, not a punishment mechanic.
- Piggies can react to completed tasks, check-ins, photos, and reviews.
- Instead of public donation, the meaning is private: our memories, dates, cooking, chores, and savings become visible love.

### Baba Farm

Useful pattern:

- Crops grow through stages.
- Seasonal skins and map themes keep the same loop fresh.
- Co-planting expresses relationships.

Piggy Days adaptation:

- Seeds grow flowers, vegetables, or fruit trees in stages.
- Farm themes can follow seasons, holidays, or Perth weather.
- Couple quests can unlock shared decorations.

### Hay Day

Useful pattern:

- A farm feels richer when it has zones, animals, visitors, decorations, and unlockable areas.
- Decoration is not just cosmetic. It helps the farm feel owned.

Piggy Days adaptation:

- Keep the core farm small, but make it spatial: piggy pen, house, garden, market stall, picnic area, memory wall.
- Let rewards unlock visible objects rather than abstract numbers only.

### Stardew Valley

Useful pattern:

- Long-term progression comes from collections, bundles, and new areas.
- The player can progress through different activities, not one forced path.

Piggy Days adaptation:

- Use memory collections and couple milestones instead of heavy RPG systems.
- Shopping, cooking, city outings, chores, and dates can each fill different collections.

## Product Fit

Piggy Farm is not a standalone game. It is the emotional reward layer for Piggy Days.

The app should always answer:

- What did we do in real life?
- What did it add to our farm?
- What memory did it leave behind?
- What small thing should we do next?

## Core Fantasy

Two piggies live on a small private farm. They grow happier as we do small real-life things together.

The farm slowly fills with:

- Food we cooked.
- Flowers from check-ins.
- Coins from grocery savings.
- Decorations from dates and outings.
- Memory cards from weekly reviews.

## Game Pillars

### 1. Soft Care

Piggies can be fed, dressed, and cheered up.

Rules:

- No death.
- No punishment for missing days.
- If we are inactive, piggies nap or wait.
- The game should feel forgiving.

### 2. Visible Growth

Every meaningful real-life action should create a visible change:

- Complete task: feed bowl fills.
- Add check-in photo: seed sprouts.
- Complete outing: new flower blooms.
- Save money on groceries: coin jar fills.
- Generate review: memory card appears.

### 3. Couple Co-op

The game is built for two people, not a public social network.

Examples:

- "Cook together" quest.
- "Saturday mini outing" quest.
- "Deal hunter" shopping quest.
- "Home reset" chore quest.
- "Try again" quest from a review memory.

### 4. Personal Memory

The farm should remember what happened.

Examples:

- A flower can be linked to a check-in.
- A decoration can show the task that unlocked it.
- A weekly review can become a framed card on the memory wall.

### 5. Tiny Surprise

Piggy Farm should have small delightful moments:

- Piggy dialogue after completing a task.
- Random weather matching the day.
- Seasonal decorations.
- A tiny animation when feeding, planting, or collecting coins.

## Core Loop

1. Plan something: task, shopping list, outing, or review.
2. Do it in real life.
3. Check in with note, cost, place, or photo.
4. Earn Feed, Seeds, Coins, and sometimes Hearts.
5. Spend resources on care, planting, or decorations.
6. Farm updates visually.
7. Piggies react with a short line.
8. Weekly review turns progress into memory cards and next quests.

## Resources

### Feed

Earned from:

- Completing any task.
- Generating a review.

Used for:

- Feeding piggies.
- Starting simple piggy actions.

### Seeds

Earned from:

- Check-ins.
- Photos.
- Outings.

Used for:

- Planting flowers.
- Growing garden crops.
- Unlocking memory garden items.

### Coins

Earned from:

- Estimated grocery savings.
- Completing shopping quests.

Used for:

- Decorations.
- House upgrades.
- Market stall upgrades.

### Hearts

Earned from:

- Couple quests.
- Date tasks.
- Weekly review streaks.
- Doing something suggested by a past review.

Used for:

- Relationship decorations.
- Piggy outfits.
- Special memory frames.

V1 can keep Hearts hidden or postpone them. Feed, Seeds, and Coins are enough for the first local milestone.

## Piggies

V1 has two piggies.

Each piggy can have:

- Name.
- Mood.
- Favorite food.
- Favorite activity.
- Outfit slot.
- Short dialogue line.

Simple moods:

- Happy.
- Hungry.
- Sleepy.
- Proud.
- Curious.
- Celebrating.

Mood should be based on recent activity, not strict timers.

Example dialogue:

- "We saved a little today. Coin jar time."
- "That check-in photo smells like a good memory."
- "One task done. I am pretending I helped."
- "The garden wants one more seed."
- "This week deserves a tiny flower."

## Farm Areas

### 1. Piggy Pen

Main care area.

V1:

- Shows two piggies.
- Feed bowl.
- Mood bubble.

Later:

- Outfits.
- Small toys.
- Nap blanket.

### 2. Cottage

The home base.

V1:

- Static house.

Later:

- Upgrade roof, window, mailbox, lights.
- Display seasonal skins.

### 3. Garden Patch

For Seeds.

V1:

- Plant simple sprouts and flowers.

Later:

- Staged growth: seed, sprout, bloom, harvest.
- Crops linked to cooking tasks.

### 4. Coin Jar

For grocery savings.

V1:

- Shows Coins.
- Fills visually.

Later:

- Unlocks market stall decorations.
- Shows "saved this month" milestone.

### 5. Memory Wall

For reviews and check-ins.

V1:

- Shows latest memory cards.

Later:

- Filter by date, place, category.
- Frames unlocked by Hearts.

### 6. Market Stall

For shopping gameplay.

V1:

- Not required.

Later:

- Shows mock deals as cute stall items.
- Rewards shopping list completion.

### 7. Picnic Corner

For outings and dates.

V1:

- Not required.

Later:

- Unlocks after first city outing.
- Displays outing badges and route cards.

## Progression

Keep progression simple and visible.

### Farm Level

Farm XP comes from farm events:

- Complete task: +5 XP.
- Check-in with note: +5 XP.
- Check-in with photo: +10 XP.
- Complete city outing: +20 XP.
- Generate review: +15 XP.

Levels unlock areas:

- Level 1: Piggy Pen, Cottage, Feed Bowl.
- Level 2: Garden Patch.
- Level 3: Memory Wall.
- Level 4: Coin Jar visual upgrade.
- Level 5: Market Stall.
- Level 6: Picnic Corner.
- Level 7+: Seasonal themes and special decorations.

### Collections

Collections give long-term goals without pressure.

Examples:

- Home Helpers: complete chore tasks.
- Tiny Dates: complete date tasks.
- Perth Weekends: complete city outings.
- Kitchen Wins: complete cooking tasks.
- Deal Hunter: save money from shopping.
- Photo Flowers: add check-in photos.

Each collection can unlock a decoration or memory frame.

## Daily and Weekly Rhythm

### Daily 30-Second Loop

When opening the app:

1. See piggies and farm state.
2. Collect any pending rewards.
3. Feed piggies if Feed is available.
4. Plant one Seed if available.
5. See one suggested tiny task.

### Weekly 5-Minute Loop

At the end of the week:

1. Generate review.
2. See completed tasks, places, spending, and savings.
3. Pick one memory card.
4. Choose one suggested quest for next week.
5. Unlock or progress one decoration.

## Quest Types

### Tiny Task Quest

Short everyday tasks.

Examples:

- Buy milk.
- Clean one area.
- Cook one simple dinner.
- Walk after dinner.

Reward:

- Feed +1.
- XP +5.

### Check-In Quest

Tasks with a note or photo.

Examples:

- Try a new cafe.
- Cook something we have not cooked before.
- Visit a small market.

Reward:

- Feed +1.
- Seeds +1.
- XP +10.

### Couple Quest

Tasks meant for both people.

Examples:

- "One-hour Saturday date."
- "Cook together."
- "Pick a small home improvement."
- "Find one good grocery deal."

Reward:

- Feed +1.
- Seeds +1.
- Hearts +1.
- XP +15.

### Shopping Quest

Grocery-related tasks.

Examples:

- Finish a shopping list.
- Buy three deal items we actually need.
- Save at least $5.

Reward:

- Coins equal to whole-dollar savings.
- XP +10.

### Review Quest

Generated from memory review.

Examples:

- "Do this place again."
- "Repeat last week's best dinner."
- "Try a cheaper version of this shop."

Reward:

- Feed +1.
- Memory card.
- XP +15.

## MVP Scope

The first playable version should include:

- Two piggies on one farm screen.
- Feed, Seeds, Coins counters.
- Recent rewards.
- Simple piggy mood.
- Feed action.
- Garden planting action.
- Memory wall with mock or generated review cards.
- Farm event log.
- Reward animation for completing a task.
- A small set of unlockable decorations.

Keep out of the first playable version:

- Real-time decay.
- Push reminders.
- Complex crop timers.
- Multiplayer beyond two local users.
- Public social features.
- Paid items.
- Random loot boxes.
- Heavy economy balancing.

## Data Model Notes

Likely future models:

- `FarmState`: resource totals, level, XP, selected theme.
- `FarmEvent`: immutable reward and unlock events.
- `Piggy`: name, mood, outfit, favorite.
- `FarmDecoration`: unlocked item, placed area, source event.
- `FarmPlant`: type, growth stage, source check-in.
- `MemoryCard`: already planned as review output.
- `Quest`: generated or manual next action.

For MVP, avoid too many tables. `FarmEvent` plus derived farm summary can carry most of the loop.

## UI Direction

Mood:

- Cozy.
- Personal.
- Slightly playful.
- Warm, but not babyish.

Visual ideas:

- Isometric or soft 2.5D farm canvas.
- Two piggies near a small cottage.
- Farm areas as tappable zones.
- Gentle idle animation.
- Small speech bubbles.
- Decorative progress, not dense dashboards.

Important:

- The farm should be the first screen, not a stats page.
- Numbers should support the scene, not dominate it.
- Mobile should feel like opening a tiny private toy.

## Implementation Order

### Step 1: Farm Summary

- Show resources.
- Show recent farm events.
- Show two piggies.
- Add simple mood rules.

### Step 2: Task Completion Reward

- Completing a task creates farm events.
- Farm summary updates.
- Piggies react.

### Step 3: Planting and Feeding

- Feed button consumes Feed and changes mood.
- Plant button consumes Seeds and creates a plant event.

### Step 4: Memory Wall

- Review cards appear on farm.
- Cards link back to date range.

### Step 5: Unlocks

- XP and level.
- First set of decorations.
- Garden and memory wall unlock states.

## Open Questions

- Should the two piggies represent the two of us directly, or be two shared mascots?
- Should Hearts be visible in v1, or saved for couple quests later?
- Should decorations be placed manually, or auto-placed first?
- Should farm progression be level-based, collection-based, or both?

Recommended answers for v1:

- Treat piggies as shared mascots.
- Hide Hearts until couple quests exist.
- Auto-place decorations first.
- Use level-based unlocks first, collections later.
